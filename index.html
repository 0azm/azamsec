Flagship · Live tool

GPU Password-Cracking Benchmark & Auditor

Aug 2026 · KAUST Academy (HPC)

I built a password-auditing platform that runs on the KAUST Ibex supercomputer, measuring exactly how fast real hardware cracks different kinds of password protection, then turning the results into a plain-English security report with hardening advice mapped to the national cybersecurity controls.

On 3 NVIDIA V100 GPUs, a weakly stored 8-character password falls in about 15 minutes. The same password stored properly would take thousands of years.

I also made a free browser tool so anyone can test their own password's strength. It runs entirely on your device and never sends anything anywhere.
